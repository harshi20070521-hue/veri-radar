What VeriRadar does
- Checks website links against Google Safe Browsing’s known threat data.
- Reviews pasted job offers for common scam warning signs, such as requests for fees or sensitive information.
- Explains the result and suggests a safer next step.
How to use it
Choose Link Check to review a website address, or Company & Job Check to review a job link or offer message.
A note about results
VeriRadar helps identify possible warning signs. A result cannot guarantee that a website, company, or job offer is genuine or fraudulent. Always verify job openings through the company’s official website.
